# For the Slate Auto team

An affordable EV can't spend much on thermal management. This repo quantifies cheap, passive thermal choices:

- Summer battery temperature, aging rate and derated hours vs exterior coating, from a full year of real Dallas weather. ([figure](figures/battery_summer_hist.png))
- A film-on-substrate model showing which wraps or coatings reduce solar heat gain on an opaque body. That matters for a vehicle designed around customizable wraps. ([design map](figures/laminate_design_map.png))

**A 3-week project I could do for you, remote:** a parked-vehicle cabin and pack soak model, comparing wrap colors and coatings by peak cabin and pack temperature in hot-climate markets.
