# For the Varda team

`radiator.py` covers on-orbit heat rejection (area vs temperature for any α/ε coating, in LEO hot and shaded cases). The rest of the repo is the same radiative surface balance used for terrestrial cooling, with real-weather validation.

**A 3-week project I could do for you, remote:** a capsule on-orbit thermal model (hot/cold cases, coating trade) to bound payload temperatures during the manufacturing phase of a mission.
