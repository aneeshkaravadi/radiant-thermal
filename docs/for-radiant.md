# For the Radiant team

A containerized reactor has to shed heat through its container surfaces in every climate. This repo has the pieces:

- a surface energy balance (sun, sky IR, ground IR, convection)
- a two-node transient model
- a full year of real hourly Texas weather

All with α and ε as explicit design inputs.

**A 3-week project I could do for you, remote:** a weather-resolved heat-rejection and container-skin-temperature model for candidate deployment sites, ranking coatings and sun-shading options.
