# For the Aalo Atomics team

The relevant parts are heat rejection by radiation and convection with explicit surface properties, run on real hourly weather:

- A surface energy balance with sky emissivity from dew point and cloud cover, and a convection coefficient from wind, over a full year of Texas weather.
- A design map of how surface α and ε change equilibrium temperature.
