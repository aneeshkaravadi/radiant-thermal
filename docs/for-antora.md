# For the Antora Energy team

Your thermal batteries run hot enough that radiation dominates heat transfer, so the core of this repo, radiative exchange with explicit α and ε, applies directly:

- A surface energy balance with Kirchhoff-consistent infrared exchange, sky and ground view factors, and convection.
- A film-on-substrate optics model (a geometric series of internal reflections) for laminated or coated surfaces.
- The same balance applied to a radiating panel in vacuum (`radiator.py`).

**A 3-week project I could do for you, remote:** a parametric heat-loss model for an insulated high-temperature enclosure, splitting radiation from conduction, with emissivity sensitivity of liner and shell surfaces.
